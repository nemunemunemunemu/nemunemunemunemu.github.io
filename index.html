Controls and Instructions
  • A / D: Move. W: Jump or swim upward. S: Swim down.
  • Left-click: Mine or attack. Right-click: Place the selected item.
  • 1–9 or click a hotbar slot to select it. Choose from the 9 slots; the selected item name appears below.
  • Hotbar: Press I, select a slot, then click a block to assign it. In Survival, the panel also has crafting recipes.
  • `: Switch foreground/background placement. In background mode, left-click removes wall tiles.
  • E: Use beach chairs, talk to villagers, or open and close doors. I: Blocks and Survival crafting.
  • Big Hands: Craft with 8 Wood and 4 Iron for longer reach; the gloves show when equipped.
  • Creative: Use the mob picker to spawn mobs. F toggles flight; use WASD to fly.
  • Space: Jump. With a Jetpack equipped, hold Space or W in the air to fly until the fuel bar runs out.
  • Items: Select an item (flamethrower, snowballs, laser, air horn, PB jar, peanuts) and hold right-click to use it.
  • TNT: Left-click placed TNT to light it. Flames and explosions light nearby TNT too.
  • Launch Pad: Stand on one and press E to fly to Mars (or back home). There's a spaceport at the far east end of the tundra.
  • Bosses: Mine the Ant Egg, Frozen Idol, Golden Egg, Golden Jar or Alien Beacon to wake what's guarding it.
  • Enter: Chat. Type /help for commands. Tab: Player list. M: Map size.
  • Esc: Pause or resume. In multiplayer the world keeps going while this menu is open.
